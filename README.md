# Personal Manifestation: Mayow — policies

Privacy policy, terms of service and account deletion instructions for
**Personal Manifestation: Mayow** (`com.astraapp.astra`), published by
Tetradevelopers and served by GitHub Pages so the Play Console and the app can link
to stable URLs.

- Privacy policy — https://sid-sys.github.io/policies/privacy.html
- Terms of service — https://sid-sys.github.io/policies/terms.html
- Delete your account — https://sid-sys.github.io/policies/delete-account.html

These are the copies Google reads. The app links to the same URLs from its
Profile and paywall screens, via `lib/services/legal_links.dart` in the app
repository — change a URL here and change it there in the same breath, because
a policy link that 404s is a listing rejection at the next review.

## Every page must name the app

Google rejected the listing once over this. The app was renamed from Astra to
Personal Manifestation: Mayow, these pages were not, and the account deletion
link was refused for "missing reference to the app or to the entity named in
the app's Google Play listing" — the page loaded fine, it just named the wrong
app.

So every page here states the listing name, the package id and the publisher
near the top. If the store listing is ever renamed again, rename it here in the
same hour.

## Keep the claims true

These pages are read against the app, not on their own. The terms said Plus
included twenty stories a month when the app gave ten manifestations; a price
or an allowance that disagrees with the app is the Misleading Claims section of
Play policy. When the cap, the price or a feature changes in the app, it
changes here too.
