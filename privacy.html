Personal Manifestation: Mayow — published by Tetradevelopers

Mayow writes personalised audio manifestations from things you tell us about your life. That only works if you hand over something real, so this page says exactly what we take, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Your first name It is spoken inside your manifestations. Taken from your Google account if you sign in that way, otherwise you type it.
What you want to manifest — every wish you type It is what gets written. This is the most personal thing here, and it is treated that way.
Your answers to the four questions Mayow reads your wish, writes four questions about it, and writes from what you pick.
Your email address To sign you in and to let you recover your account.
The manifestations and affirmations written for you — words and audio So your library is there on your next phone.
How many manifestations you have generated, and when To apply your plan's monthly limit.
A purchase receipt, if you subscribe To confirm with Google Play or the Apple App Store that a subscription is real. We never see your card — Google or Apple handles payment and we never receive card details.

Your profile photo never leaves your phone

If you add one, it is stored on the device and nowhere else. It is not uploaded, not backed up, and not visible to us. It disappears when you uninstall the app.

Trial reminder

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, we send you one notification the day before it ends, with a way to cancel in Google Play or in your Apple ID settings. We use your push address for that, and for your daily affirmations if you turn them on, and for nothing else.

About your phone and how you found us

To count installs, the app sends our server a random install number it makes on your phone, the date your phone says the app was installed, the date it was last opened, and the push address Google or Apple gives your phone. These are tied to your account while you are signed in. A daily check asks Google (or Apple) whether the push address still works (nothing is sent to your phone) so we know if the app was removed. We also keep the days you opened the app, the first time you reached each step (for example finishing the questions), and, if you tell us, where you heard about Mayow. If you send feedback or a bug report, we keep what you wrote and which account sent it. We use all of this to improve Mayow, never for advertising, and it is deleted with your account.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifiers. No location. No contacts. No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and there is nothing in the app that follows you around the internet.

Who else sees it

Writing a manifestation means sending some of what you told us to the companies whose models produce it. They act as our processors, under contracts that forbid using your data to train their models.

WhoWhat they receive
Anthropic (Claude) Your first name, your four answers, the area of life you chose, and the wish you typed — in order to write it.
OpenAI Your first name, your four answers, the area of life you chose and the wish you typed, in order to write your manifestation, your questions and your affirmations. They do not receive your email or your account.
ElevenLabs The finished manifestation text, in order to turn it into audio. They do not receive your name separately, your email, or your account.
Google (Firebase) Hosts the accounts, the database, the audio files and the code that writes your manifestations.
Google Play and the Apple App Store Handles the subscription and tells us only whether a receipt is valid and when it expires.

That is the entire list. Nobody else receives your data, and no part of it is sold or shared with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Your account, your writing and your audio are stored on Google Cloud servers in India (asia-south1). If you are using Mayow from outside India, your information is transferred to and stored there. Our model providers process requests in the United States and other countries where they operate.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry, because your library is the point of the app. When you delete your account we remove your profile, your manifestations, your audio files and your account itself — see deleting your account. Deletion is immediate and permanent; we keep no backup copy you could ask us to restore.

Your choices

  • Tell us only what you want written about. The app asks for a first name and what you are calling in. Nothing else about you is required.
  • See everything we hold. Your profile, library and affirmations are all visible in the app.
  • Delete everything, yourself, in two taps. Profile → Delete my data.

Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct, export or restrict the processing of your information, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to us and we will help.

Children

Mayow is not for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit and data is encrypted at rest by Google Cloud. Access rules on our database mean one account cannot read another's writing. No system is perfect, and we will tell you promptly if something happens that affects your data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who receives it, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions, requests, or anything that looks wrong: support@coreinteractive.live.
