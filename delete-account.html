Delete your Personal Manifestation: Mayow account

Personal Manifestation: Mayow — com.astraapp.astra — published by Tetradevelopers

You can delete your Mayow account and everything in it yourself, from inside the app, in about ten seconds. You do not need to ask us and you do not need to explain.

From the app

  1. Open Mayow and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom, to Account.
  3. Tap Delete my data.
  4. Read what will go, type DELETE to confirm, and tap Delete everything.

What is deleted

  • Your account itself, and the ability to sign in with it
  • Your name and anything else you told the app about yourself
  • Every manifestation written for you — the words and the audio files
  • Every affirmation written for you
  • Every story written for you under the app's earlier name
  • Your usage history and streak
  • Everything stored on your phone by the app, including your profile photo

This is immediate and permanent. There is no recycle bin and no backup we can restore from. If you want to keep a manifestation, save it before you delete.

What is kept, and for how long

If you have ever subscribed, Google Play keeps its own record of the transaction. That is Google's record, not ours, and it is kept because tax and accounting law requires it. We do not keep a copy of your personal information after deletion.

Server logs may briefly contain a request identifier for up to 30 days as part of normal operation. These are rotated automatically and are not linked back to a deleted account.

Cancel your subscription too

Deleting your Mayow account does not cancel a Google Play subscription — only Google can do that. Open the Play Store, go to Subscriptions, and cancel Mayow Plus there, otherwise it will keep renewing.

If you cannot open the app

Lost the phone, uninstalled already, or cannot sign in? Email support@coreinteractive.live from the address on the account, with the subject Delete my account. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.
